Pakistan Petrol Price Today April 2026: 42% Hike Then Partial Reversal — Complete Analysis

In less than one month, Pakistan's petrol price surged 63% — driven by the Iran war, IMF pressure, and a weakening rupee. PM Shehbaz cut Rs 80/litre but diesel remains at a record Rs 520.35.

By PakFuelWatch Editorial Desk  |  April 4, 2026  |  6 min read

Pakistan is experiencing one of its most severe fuel price crises in history. In under 30 days, petrol prices surged over 63% — a shock driven by the ongoing US-Iran conflict disrupting Strait of Hormuz oil shipments, IMF conditions capping fuel subsidies, and the Pakistani rupee's continued weakness against the US dollar.

On April 3, 2026, the government raised petrol from Rs 321 to Rs 458.40 per litre — a single-day jump of 42.7%. Diesel soared from Rs 335 to Rs 520.35, a 55% increase. After intense public backlash, PM Shehbaz Sharif announced a Rs 80/litre reduction the very next day, bringing petrol to Rs 378.41 — where it stands today.

"It was inevitable to raise the prices due to international market prices going out of control after the US-Iran war."

— Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik, April 3, 2026

Why Did Prices Spike? The Three Root Causes

Pakistan imports most of its fuel from Saudi Arabia and the UAE, transported through the Strait of Hormuz. The US-Iran conflict created severe disruption in this vital shipping route, sending international crude prices up by over 20% almost overnight.

The second factor is the Pakistani Rupee's weakness — since oil is globally traded in US dollars, every drop in the rupee automatically increases PKR costs. The third cause: IMF conditions capping subsidies at Rs 152 billion, preventing the government from absorbing more of the price shock.

Impact on the Public — Who Is Affected Most?

Fuel prices are directly linked to the cost of everything — from bus fares to vegetable prices. When diesel rises 55%, truckers pass costs to consumers. Pakistan's agriculture sector (24% of GDP) is particularly exposed given its heavy reliance on diesel.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is petrol price in Pakistan today April 2026?

As of April 4, 2026, petrol (MS) price in Pakistan is Rs 378.41 per litre — after PM Shehbaz Sharif reduced it by Rs 80 from the record high of Rs 458.40.

What is diesel price in Pakistan today?

High Speed Diesel (HSD) price is Rs 520.35 per litre — the highest in Pakistan's history. No diesel reduction has been announced.

Why did Pakistan petrol price increase so much?

Three main reasons: (1) US-Iran conflict disrupted Strait of Hormuz oil shipping. (2) The weakening Rupee increased import costs in PKR. (3) IMF conditions capped subsidies at Rs 152 billion.

When will petrol prices decrease in Pakistan?

This depends on international oil markets and the Iran conflict. PM's Rs 80 cut is pledged for at least 1 month. OGRA reviews prices every 15 days — next review around April 15–16.

Is petrol price the same across all Pakistan?

Yes — OGRA sets one national price. Rs 378.41/litre applies equally in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Faisalabad, Peshawar and all other cities.

How to get the motorbike fuel subsidy?

Rs 100/litre subsidy for motorbike users (max 20L/month, 3 months). The exact disbursement mechanism has not yet been officially announced. Check back for updates.
